What Crux Does

Everything your server needs

From basic moderation to advanced AI-powered commands — Crux does it all.

🔨
Full Moderation Suite
Ban, kick, timeout, warn, purge, lock, slowmode and more. Every action is logged to your mod-log channel with full executor info.
🤖
AI Assistant
Describe what you want in plain English — the bot does it. "Timeout @user 10m for spam and purge their last 20 messages."
⭐ PREMIUM
🛡️
AutoMod
Word filter, anti-spam, mention limits, caps filter, and invite/link blocker. Fully configurable with your own word lists.

🔒
Jail System
Isolate a member instantly — removes all their roles, assigns a jail role, and restores everything on release. Full audit log.

Temp Role
Assign a role temporarily — it's automatically removed after the set duration even if the bot restarts.
🔇
Mass Timeout
Timeout all humans or members with a specific role in one command. Perfect for raid situations.

Join-to-Create Channels
Members join a hub channel and instantly get their own private voice channel. They own it — rename, lock, set limits, kick members, or delete it via /j2cpanel.

🎫
Ticket System
Button-based support tickets with full transcripts on close. Multiple panels for different support topics.

Giveaways

Community & XP
Chat XP, leveling, leaderboards, daily coin rewards and full member profiles to keep your community thriving.

Reaction Roles
